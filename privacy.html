Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This is a simple school website about Belarus. The good news is: the site collects almost no data at all. Please read this short policy to understand why you can browse it safely.

1. Information we collect

This website is static. It does not ask you to register, log in or fill in any forms. We do not collect your name, your e-mail address or any other personal information. In fact, this site has no way to collect anything from you — there are simply no registration or contact forms.

2. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking, and it does not store anything on your device. There are no advertising banners and no analytics code.

3. Log files

Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may keep short technical logs of visits — for example the IP address, the browser type and the pages that were opened. This information is used by the hosting provider to keep the site working and secure, and it is never used by the author of this website.

4. Third-party content

All photographs on this website are taken from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses. The images are loaded together with the site, so no third-party cookies are used. Links to other websites (for example, the author's GitHub profile or Wikimedia Commons) lead to pages with their own privacy policies.

5. Children's privacy

This website was made for an English school project. It is safe for children: it does not ask children for any personal data and contains no inappropriate content.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change from time to time. If it does, the “last updated” date at the top of this page will be changed too.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the author on GitHub: timgihangir-rgb.
